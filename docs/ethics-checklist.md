# Ethics Checklist & Data Provenance

## Ethical Constraints (from Proposal & AGENTS.md)
- [x] No live participant interaction. Retrospective text and controlled simulations only.
- [x] No method or means details in generated text.
- [x] No clinical effectiveness claims. "Insufficient evidence" is an allowed/expected result.
- [x] Examples in the paper must be paraphrased and stripped of identifying details.
- [x] Generated conversations must avoid graphic content.
